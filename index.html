Civil Unrest is a strategy board game that blends the territorial conquest of Risk with the economic engine of Monopoly. How to Play

  1. Claim Territory

    Place your forces on the board and establish your starting positions. Location is everything.

  2. Build Your Economy

    Acquire resources, invest in infrastructure, and generate income to fund your campaigns.

  3. Forge & Break Alliances

    Negotiate with other players — trade, treaties, or threats. Trust is a currency you can spend.

  4. Conquer & Control

    Expand your territory through strategic combat. But overextend and the people may rise against you.

  5. Win the Game

    Achieve dominance through military might, economic supremacy, or political control. Multiple paths to victory.
