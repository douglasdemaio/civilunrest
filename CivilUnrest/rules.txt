	 	 	 	
*A Game of Fragile Stability*
Objective
In Civil Unrest, you play as the leader of a nation navigating modern crises. Your goal is to survive politically, economically, socially as long as possible. The game ends when one player remains under the collapse threshold or when a player achieves a reform victory.
Setup
    • Unfold the Civil Unrest board in the center.
    • Each player starts with their Unrest Meter at 0 (0–100 sliding scale).
    • Shuffle the event cards and place each on its matching stack.
    • All players place their pawn on 	the START space.
    • The person who is the youngest player starts.
1. Roll Dice: Move forward the number of spaces rolled.
2. Resolve the Board Space:
 - Each board space has a category (color) and a base point value.
 - When landing, draw the top card from the corresponding deck.
3. Resolve the Card:
 - Add the points from the card to the space’s value. The term on the card (Use Card Value) is simply the space value. Some cards might have a + or - on the card. The minus can be used to reduce the space value. For example if the space value is +4 and your card says -2, the unrest meter will go up by two points. If the card is +2 and the space value says +1, the unrest meter will go up by three points. 
 - If the card has a multiplier, apply it to the board value first.
 - If the card is a reform or token-reducing card, reduce your total unrest by that amount.
 - Some cards award points to another player/s. Unless the card also says to take or award circle points, the player who landed on the space gets the space value.
Special Board Spaces
 - +→ Spaces: Each color has one +→ space. The first player to land on it may buy it for 10 unrest points; after that, whenever anyone lands there, the owner may give another player 5 unrest points. If the first player doesn’t buy it, anyone who lands there afterward may give another player 2 unrest points.
 - Civil Unrest Logo Spaces: Landing on a logo space draws no card and triggers no event — it is an unrest relief space.
 - Fist Spaces (Civil Unrest: America expansion): A fist space (Civil Unrest Logo Space) activates the America expansion deck or any other expansion deck used. If you are not playing the expansion, treat a fist space as a logo (relief) space.

How Points Work
All unrest is tracked using with your personal Unrest Meter (0–100). You must add unrest when required unless a card negates, reduces, or offsets it. Players can not go in to negative points, so players can not each negative unrest (capped at 0).

Reform Mechanics
Red cards are rare but powerful. If you land on a red space or draw a red card, it may reduce your unrest, prevent collapse, or offer strategic relief. Some reform cards may be held and played later — if they say so.
Collapse & Elimination
If a player’s Unrest Meter reaches 100 or more, their government collapses. They are eliminated from the game.
Victory Conditions
Standard Victory: Be the last player whose unrest has not reached 100.
Reform Victory (Optional Rule): If a player reduces their unrest to under 20 and has drawn 3 red reform cards in a row, they may declare a Reform Victory and immediately win.
Tyrant Victory (Optional Rule): If a player gains +25 unrest within 3 consecutive turns, they win instantly.
Optional Rules
    • Crisis Mode: Every 5 rounds, all players must draw a random Yellow card (climate/disaster).
    • Two Dice Economy: Allow players to roll two dice, using either total or highest die only to move, adding strategic pacing.
    • Player Sabotage: Purple cards may allow unrest to be transferred or forced onto others.
    • Civil Unrest: America: Mix the America expansion deck into the color decks and activate the board's fist spaces.
    • If the Civil Unrest logo appears on the back of the card next to the situation, the player may pass the card to the next player without gaining any points from it.
Closing Note
Civic collapse isn’t inevitable, but it is inevitable in this game if you don’t manage it wisely. Draw, adapt, and reform your way to stability… or be the last nation standing when others fall.
